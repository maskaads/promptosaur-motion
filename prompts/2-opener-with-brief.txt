Make a 15-second motion graphics opener for a YouTube channel called Promptosaur. Final file: final.mp4, 1920x1080, 30 fps, with sound.

About the channel: tutorials and tests where AI models make motion graphics, animation and 3D. The audience is creators aged 18 to 35 who watch on a phone. The mascot is a small purple dinosaur at a laptop.

Look:
- Colors: background #14101F, purple #7C4DFF, yellow #FFD23F, off-white #F4F1EA. No other colors.
- One font family for everything, a bold geometric sans. Two sizes only.
- Flat vector shapes. No photos. No gradients on text.

Motion:
- Every move eases out: fast start, long settle. No linear moves.
- No two elements start on the same frame. Offset them by 2 to 4 frames.
- After each move, hold at least 12 frames so the text can be read.
- A cut or a big change every 1.5 to 2.5 seconds.

Structure:
- 0 to 3 s: a command-line cursor blinks, then types the word "prompt".
- 3 to 9 s: the word turns into three quick scenes: a motion graphics card, a 2D character, a 3D object. Two seconds each.
- 9 to 13 s: the mascot dinosaur forms out of the shapes of the last scene.
- 13 to 15 s: the name PROMPTOSAUR, one small final move, then a still hold.

Sound: a short music bed that you make yourself in code, plus a sound effect on every cut and every text hit.

Work in this order:
1. Pick your tools yourself. Start from this empty folder.
2. Build a first draft of the whole video.
3. Export 8 frames from each scene as one contact sheet image and look at it.
4. List what looks wrong: overlaps, text off screen, moves at one speed, empty frames.
5. Fix the list, then render final.mp4.

No AI image or video generators. Make everything with code.

Do not ask me questions. Work until final.mp4 exists and plays.
